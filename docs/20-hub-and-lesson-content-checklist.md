## Reading rules and media profiles

- Coverage states: **PROVEN** = current artifact plus learner wiring and focused evidence; **PARTIAL** = useful current slice but the stated requirement is not complete; **MISSING** = no useful learner implementation; **BLOCKED** = a named human/rights/data gate prevents release.
- Publication states are taken from current fragments, not old reports. `published/source-only` means the object is published in a source fragment but intentionally absent from the learner projection.
- Current media inventory: 9 public illustrations, 3 public SVG infographics, 15 public owner-approved workbook MP3s, 54 technically-passing generated TTS preview candidates that remain human-review blocked, and an internal 15-track transcript projection that is not UI-wired and has no separately recorded transcript-publication right.
- `G-ACT` media gate: correct activity-to-asset mapping; informative `alt` plus selectable text equivalent; responsive crop/pan at 360/390/820/1440; keyboard/reduced-motion check; no graphic used as the only answer.
- `G-WORD` media gate: article/gender/form must remain selectable text with non-color cue; a meaning image is optional unless the activity requires picture matching; any image must be target-specific and have reviewed alt text.
- `A-TTS` media gate: exact spoken text/concept mapping, checksum/codec gate, two qualified German listening approvals, source/generated label, Pages-safe public path, shared speed/repeat/error controls, and no scoring claim.
- `A-WB` media gate: one of the exact owner-approved tracks `1_01`–`1_15`, byte/hash match, correct exercise and route mapping, source badge, pitch-preserving speed/repeat, and a lawful post-attempt transcript or equivalent accessible path.
- `T-VISIBLE` means the German learning text itself is the required text alternative; `T-POST` means a listening transcript/caption/equivalent must become available after the attempt without disclosing the answer beforehand; `T-NA` means no time-based media is required.
