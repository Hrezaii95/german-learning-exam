# Lessons 1–2 Content Specification

This is the Alpha curriculum contract. It is derived from the official English glossary and the owned coursebook/workbook. Exact publication objects still require assertion-level encoding and review.

## Lesson 1 — `Ich heiße Miriam.`

### Communicative outcome

The learner can greet and say goodbye, introduce self/others, ask and answer names, spell a name, say origin, ask/respond about wellbeing, and choose `du` or formal `Sie` in the taught contexts.

### Official sequence

Coursebook printed pages 10–14, exercises 1–8; Workbook printed pages 6–9, exercises 1–15; English glossary Lesson 01.

### Core content groups

**Greetings/farewells:** `Hallo`, `Guten Tag`, `Guten Morgen`, `Guten Abend`, `Gute Nacht`, `Tschüs`, `Auf Wiedersehen`.

**Wellbeing:** `Wie geht’s dir?`, `Wie geht’s Ihnen?`, `Super!`, `Sehr gut, danke.`, `Gut, danke.`, `Es geht.`, `Nicht so gut.`, `Und dir?`, `Und Ihnen?`, `Auch super.`

**Name/identity:** `Wie heißt du?`, `Wie heißen Sie?`, `Und wer bist du?`, `Wer sind Sie?`, `Wer ist das?`, `Ich heiße …`, `Mein Name ist …`, `Ich bin …`, `Das ist …`, `der Name`, `der Vorname`, `der Familienname`, `Herr`, `Frau`, `Entschuldigung`, `Wie bitte?`, `Danke`.

**Origin/countries:** `Woher kommst du?`, `Woher kommen Sie?`, `Ich komme aus …`, `Eritrea`, `Deutschland`, `die Schweiz`, `die Türkei`, `Spanien`, `Frankreich`, `Österreich`, `die USA`; contextual `Südafrika` appears in glossary exercise 6 and must be classified core/non-core using formatting evidence.

**Alphabet:** A–Z, Ä, Ö, Ü, ß; spelling a first and family name.

**Pronouns:** `ich`, `du`, `er`, `sie` singular, `Sie` formal.

**Verbs:**

| Verb | Lesson forms required | Notice |
|---|---|---|
| `sein` | ich bin, du bist, er/sie ist, Sie sind | irregular; formal/plural form |
| `heißen` | ich heiße, du heißt, er/sie heißt, Sie heißen | ß and form contrast |
| `kommen` | ich komme, du kommst, er/sie kommt, Sie kommen | regular endings |
| `lernen` | ich lerne, du lernst, er/sie lernt, Sie lernen | regular endings |

**Grammar:** statement position 2; W-question word + finite verb + person; conjugation singular plus formal `Sie`; `aus` and `aus der/den` for article-bearing countries; `du/Sie` register; linking with `und`.

### Required lesson activities

1. greetings-by-context visual + hotspot audio;
2. greeting/farewell picture/audio match;
3. name model dialogue;
4. alphabet listen-and-spell;
5. `heißen` vs `sein` notice card;
6. wellbeing scale with phrase audio;
7. origin map/cards and `aus` contrast;
8. pronoun-person map and singular verb builder;
9. casual/formal Q&A builder;
10. verified workbook listening for Exercises 3 and 9;
11. guided introduction conversation recording;
12. mixed checkpoint and two-minute summary.

### Lesson 1 checkpoint evidence

- recognize all core greetings/farewells from text/audio;
- spell a supplied name;
- build at least one casual and one formal name/origin question;
- conjugate taught singular/formal forms in context;
- complete an introduction dialogue without visible full-model text;
- route all errors into review.

## Lesson 2 — `Was macht ihr beruflich?`

### Communicative outcome

The learner can give a basic personal profile, understand and say numbers 0–100, discuss age/residence/relationship/children, ask and answer about profession, describe work with `sein`, `arbeiten als/bei` and related patterns, and form masculine/feminine profession words where applicable.

### Official sequence

Coursebook printed pages 15–18, exercises 1–8; Workbook printed pages 10–13, exercises 1–14; English glossary Lesson 02.

### Core domains

**Personal profile:** age, origin, residence, relationship/family status, studies/job. Core nouns include `Jahr`, `Kind`, `Partner/Partnerin`, `Interview`, `Wohnort`, `Herkunft`, `Alter`, `Familienstand`, `Studium`, `Text`.

**Status:** `verheiratet`, `geschieden`, `Single`, `allein`, `zusammenleben`, `keine Kinder`, `ein/zwei Kinder`.

**Numbers:** 0–20, tens 20–100, compound number inversion, telephone-number listening.

**Work language:** `Was macht ihr beruflich?`, `Was machen Sie beruflich?`, `Was bist du von Beruf?`, `Was sind Sie von Beruf?`, `Ich bin …`, `Ich arbeite als …`, `Ich arbeite bei …`, `Ich habe einen Job/eine Stelle als …`, `Ich studiere …`, `Ich mache eine Ausbildung/ein Praktikum als/bei …`, `Ich arbeite im Moment nicht.`

**Core professions from lesson/glossary:** `Paketzusteller/in`, `Friseur/in`, `Kellner/in`, `Ingenieur/in`, `Kfz-Mechatroniker/in`, `Student/in`, `Journalist/in`, `Architekt/in`, `Arzt/Ärztin`, `Lehrer/in`, `Verkäufer/in`, `Schüler/in`, `Rentner/in`. Coursebook/profile contexts also use medicine/study and related work terms.

**Verbs:** `wohnen`, `leben`, `haben`, `sein`, `arbeiten`, `machen`, `studieren`, plus carried-forward `heißen` and `kommen`. Store full present paradigms where Lesson 2 teaches singular/plural comparison.

**Grammar:** `wir/ihr/sie/Sie`; full present endings; irregular `sein/haben`; `nicht` in taught profile statements; `als`, `bei`, `in`; professions without article after `sein` in the taught pattern; word formation `-in` with stem changes/exceptions; plural forms as lexically stored patterns.

### Teacher professions collection

The learner note contains 48 English job rows corresponding to the teacher image. All must be encoded as individual canonical lexemes and linked form groups. Categories below are for learning, not source order:

- **Trades/construction:** Elektriker, Gärtner, Automechaniker, Straßenarbeiter, Maurer, Maler, Bauarbeiter, Tischler, Klempner/Installateur.
- **Transport/service/retail:** Busfahrer, Friseur, Müllwerker, Kellner, Verkäufer, Flugzeugpilot, Polizist, Postbote/Briefträger, Sekretär, Tankwart, Putzmann/Putzfrau, Kassierer.
- **Food/agriculture:** Bäcker, Landwirt/Bauer, Koch, Metzger, Konditor.
- **Health/science:** Zahnarzt, Arzt, Krankenpfleger/Krankenschwester, Tierarzt, Chirurg.
- **Education/creative/media:** Fotograf, Architekt, Lehrer, Schauspieler, Künstler, Sänger, Schriftsteller, Journalist.
- **Technical/professional/legal:** Ingenieur, Geschäftsmann/Geschäftsfrau, Detektiv, Rechtsanwalt, Richter.
- **Public/safety/other:** Feuerwehrmann/-frau, Soldat, Geheimagent, Hausmann/Hausfrau.

Notes requiring review:

- slash alternatives become separate linked lexemes (`Landwirt` and `Bauer`, etc.);
- `Krankenpfleger` and `Krankenschwester` are a lexical pair from the assignment, not a productive suffix pair;
- `Geschäftsleute` is a collective/common plural and needs a usage note;
- `Putzmann` and some labels may be less neutral/current than alternatives; preserve the assigned form and add reviewed usage aliases rather than silently erase it;
- masculine and feminine plurals are each stored and pronounced;
- teacher image art is not shipped; original replacements are generated.

### Required Lesson 2 activities

1. personal-profile visual and fillable model;
2. full-person conjugation notice/build activities;
3. numbers 0–100 visual, build, type and telephone listening;
4. relationship/status visual and `nicht` contrasts;
5. core professions overview and individual cards;
6. person-form and plural morphology activities;
7. profession Q&A casual/formal builder and recording;
8. `sein` profession vs `arbeiten als/bei` contrast;
9. verified workbook number and word-stress listening (Exercises 6 and 12);
10. profile reading/writing activity;
11. teacher-professions assignment deck, grouped infographic, games and review;
12. mixed checkpoint and two-minute summary.

### Lesson 2 checkpoint evidence

- hear/build representative numbers and telephone strings;
- form full present paradigms for selected regular, special and irregular verbs;
- use `nicht`, `als`, `bei`, `in` in taught model sentences;
- recognize and recall the official core professions;
- demonstrate morphology on representative teacher professions across pattern families;
- complete and record a personal/work Q&A exchange;
- teacher collection mastery remains separately visible and does not block core lesson completion unless the learner marks it required.

## Content-count policy

Counts in the UI come from the validated manifest, never hard-coded prose. Core, course-context, teacher and personal counts are shown separately. The build fails if documentation totals and manifests drift after the content freeze.
