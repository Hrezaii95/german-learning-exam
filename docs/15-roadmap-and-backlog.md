# Roadmap and Backlog

## Alpha 0 — approved baseline

Complete: isolated failed Cursor demo; audited resources; approved vocabulary/Q&A/verb functional sample; selected neural audio direction; created documentation contract.

## Alpha 1 — Lessons 1–2 content foundation

- encode official glossary/core, course/workbook context and teacher-profession enrichment;
- register assertions and scope;
- align German workbook audio for relevant exercises;
- freeze content and media gap manifests.

Exit: all required objects validate and coverage report has no hidden gaps.

## Alpha 2 — visual/audio asset production

- generate Lessons 1–2 infographic family;
- generate consistent core and teacher-profession illustrations;
- generate/review de-DE word/form/sentence audio;
- package publisher listening derivatives and transcripts only after a redistribution or authenticated-use basis is recorded; until then, keep them private and ship generated models.

Exit: media QA gate passes and Cursor receives stable manifests.

## Alpha 3 — platform vertical slice

- shell/dashboard/lesson engine;
- full Lesson 1 journey;
- vocabulary/verb/grammar/phrase/listening hubs;
- event persistence and basic review.

Exit: Lesson 1 E2E works online/offline and from lesson/hub/review paths.

## Alpha 4 — Lesson 2 and enrichment

- Lesson 2 core journey;
- 48-job teacher assignment and morphology groups;
- all required games;
- Q&A/profile/conversation rehearsal;
- source listening and numbers.

Exit: core and teacher progress are separately visible; teacher deck is fully reviewable.

## Alpha 5 — mastery/release

- FSRS adapter and mixed missions;
- progress/weakness/tag/custom deck views;
- accessibility/responsive/performance polish;
- export/import and private deployment.

Exit: all release gates and independent review pass.

## Beta candidates

- validated Azure de-DE pronunciation assessment;
- guided AI conversation with constrained Lesson 1–2 intents;
- personal/teacher import UI with review workflow;
- multi-device sync and authentication;
- automated transcript word timing;
- broader pronunciation sound center/minimal pairs;
- full Module 1 (Lessons 1–3), then subsequent modules.

## Deliberately postponed

- whole A1/A1.2 ingestion and media generation;
- public course publishing or publisher-content redistribution;
- multi-user teacher portal;
- open-ended chatbot as a substitute for lesson design;
- certificates, social leaderboard or monetization;
- graph database and graph-canvas-first navigation;
- authoritative “confidence” or pronunciation scores without validated measurement.

## Enhancement backlog from sample review

- retain approved design language while adding real app shell, sources, tags and related navigation;
- route-backed detail pages rather than hash-only sample navigation;
- active-activity audio prefetch instead of preloading every course clip;
- production-portable E2E browser configuration;
- all toggle/tab radio semantics and roving keyboard focus;
- safe recorder cleanup during route change and permission races;
- configured canonical deployment metadata;
- replace emoji/CSS teaching visuals with approved original media.
