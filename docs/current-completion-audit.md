# Current objective-completion audit

Audit date: 2026-08-13  
Authority: current source, generated learner projections, manifests, executable tests, and production export behavior. Historical completion labels and the old completion matrix are not treated as proof.

## Verdict

Objective audit: **48/100 — blocked for full Alpha completion**. The repository now has a sound publication boundary, stable Lesson 1–2 routes, a useful visual/reference layer, approved workbook playback, a tested local learner-state engine, six enabled representative games, a five-step representative conversation ladder, and a functioning review shell. It is not yet the promised complete German-learning journey: the 23 published activity routes mostly render reference material rather than the activity named by the curriculum; four activities have zero published content targets; lesson Review stages and completion flow are absent; most hub records have no detail/practice/review path; teacher extras and major grammar/listening/concept surfaces are missing; and review/mastery is wired to only three representative concepts.

`proven` below means the current learner-facing behavior and its supporting contracts are demonstrated. `partial` means a useful slice exists but does not meet the stated outcome. `missing` means no learner-facing implementation was found. `blocked` means implementation also depends on an unresolved rights or human-review gate.

## Recommended packet order

1. **`COV1/COV2/COV3` — coverage reconciliation.** Publish the missing verified objects and relationships first: Lesson 2 verbs, grammar concepts, dialogues, phrase lesson ownership, listening concepts and the 48 teacher professions. Keep the existing scope/provenance gates.
2. **`J1` — activity runtime and lesson orchestrator.** Introduce typed renderers and state for the named activity, meaningful position/completion, stage progression, Check→Review and prior/next navigation.
3. **`ACT1` then `ACT2` — actual lesson interactions.** Replace generic reference-page behavior with the 23 named tasks; keep rapid sections as supporting reference material, not as the activity itself.
4. **`HUB1` + `DETAIL1` + `GAM1`.** Make every published item a living object with lesson, detail, practise, review, related and source paths; generalize games beyond the three representatives.
5. **`REV1` + `DASH1` + `REW1`.** Expand templates and cards across Lessons 1–2, persist review cursor, expose skill/weak/recent/due state, and connect tags to hub/review filtering.
6. **`MED1` + `ACT-AUD1` + `ACT-SPK1`.** Close rights/human-listening gates, add accessible transcript timing and turn playback into graded listening/speaking activities.
7. **`ONB1` + `OFF1` + `A11Y1` + `E2E1`.** Complete onboarding, offline behavior, real-browser accessibility/responsive evidence and the 12 acceptance journeys.
