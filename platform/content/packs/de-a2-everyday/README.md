# Alltagspläne: Termine und Ausflüge

An original independent A2 practice draft about arranging appointments, changing plans and organising a day trip. The A2 label describes the intended learning context; it is not a claim of certification or alignment with an official examination.

Every German string in the pack, item files and resources is **pending-german-review**. The pack and every item retain that review status. It must stay off by default until a qualified German reviewer or the owner records review. Enabling it is an explicit testing choice.

## Provenance

The vocabulary examples, sentences, dialogue, short reading, tasks, model responses and story structure were authored for this repository as a new draft. No publisher text, coursebook exercise, external recording or proprietary illustration was copied. There are no supplied audio clips, images, frequency rankings or inferred coursebook pages. `independent-a2` and lessons 1–2 name this pack's own two study units. They are not page references or a claim of affiliation with Momente or another coursebook.

## Contents and use

- 22 lexemes: 14 nouns with authored article, gender, plural and language-profile plural pattern; 8 verbs with present, preterite, participle and perfect forms.
- Four sentences with translations and authored ordering tiles.
- One grammar point with six drills: Sort, Gap, Order, Build, Transform and Error Hunt. Error Hunt asks for a replacement verb form, so the correct replacement appears only among symmetric choices.
- One short reading with three multiple-choice questions, one dialogue with a comprehension question, one speaking prompt and one writing prompt with checklists and model responses.
- Two story chapters and three timed practice sections. These are personal practice tasks, not an official exam. Writing and speaking remain self assessments without automatic correctness or pronunciation claims.

The pack uses existing language, difficulty, template, story and exam interfaces by data only. Audio-dependent and picture-dependent games remain unavailable because the draft supplies no such media. The forms `sich freuen auf` and `teilnehmen an` are practised in their example sentences. The draft includes selected forms; it does not claim to be a complete dictionary or conjugation reference.

## Review before enabling by default

Check idiom, translations, form accuracy, ambiguity of distractors, intended A2 demand and the writing model against its word-count instruction. Confirm the authored resource references and every eligible generated task. A self-review or a passing validator does not promote this draft to Reviewed.
